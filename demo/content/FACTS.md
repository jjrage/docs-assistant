## Rent and payments

- Rent due on the 1st of each month.
- Grace period: rent received by 11:59 PM on the 5th is on time.
- Late charge: $75 initial late charge on the 6th, plus $10 per day starting on the 7th until paid in full; total late charges capped at $150 per month.
- Returned payment fee: $40 per returned payment. After 2 returned payments in any 12-month period, certified funds only (cashier's check or money order) for the rest of the lease term.
- Payment methods: resident portal e-check/ACH (free); debit card ($3.95 per payment, processor fee); credit card (2.95% of the payment, processor fee); autopay by ACH (free); personal check, cashier's check or money order in the drop box beside the leasing office door, payable to "Larkfen Commons". No cash.
- Drop-box payments placed by 11:59 PM on the 5th are on time.
- Autopay: drafts on the 1st by default; resident may pick any day from the 1st to the 5th. Enroll, change or cancel at least 3 business days before the draft date. Autopay drafts the full balance due on the draft date.
- Partial payments: not accepted unless a written payment plan is signed by the Community Manager. Payment plan requests in writing by the 3rd of the month; one payment plan per 12 months. Late charges keep applying to the unpaid balance unless the written plan says otherwise.
- Proration: daily rent = monthly rent / 30.
- Staff never ask for bank account numbers, card numbers or passwords by e-mail, text or phone.
- No waiver of rent or fees is valid unless in writing and signed by the Community Manager.
