# Lease Renewal and Notice to Vacate Procedure

This procedure explains what happens as your lease end date approaches at Larkfen Commons: how renewal offers work, how to give notice if you are moving out, what happens if you do neither, and how to end a lease early. It follows the terms of your Residential Lease Agreement.

## 1. Key Dates at a Glance

| Step | Timing |
|---|---|
| Renewal offer sent to you | At least 90 days before your lease end date |
| Last day to sign a renewal lease | At least 60 days before your lease end date |
| Last day to give notice to vacate | At least 60 days before your lease end date |
| Notice to end a month-to-month lease | At least 30 days' written notice |
| Notice for early termination | At least 60 days' written notice |
| Keys due on move-out date | By 12:00 PM (noon) at the leasing office |

## 2. Renewal Offers

We send every household a renewal offer at least 90 days before the lease end date.

Renewal offers are delivered through the resident portal and by e-mail to every leaseholder.

Each renewal offer shows the new monthly rent for each available lease term.

Renewal lease terms from 6 to 15 months are offered. Terms shorter than 12 months may have a higher monthly rent.

## 3. Accepting a Renewal

To accept a renewal offer, every leaseholder must sign the renewal lease in the resident portal at least 60 days before the lease end date.

Renewal offers that are not signed at least 60 days before the lease end date expire.

To renew, your account must be paid in full and you must upload current proof of renter's insurance.

If you would like to add or remove a leaseholder at renewal, contact the leasing office before you sign. A lease change fee of $150 applies, and each new adult applicant must pay the $50 application fee and be approved.

## 4. Giving Notice to Vacate

If you do not plan to renew, you must give written notice to vacate at least 60 days before your lease end date.

Notice to vacate must be given using the Notice to Vacate form in the resident portal or a signed paper form delivered to the leasing office.

Verbal notice, text messages and phone calls are not accepted as notice to vacate.

A notice to vacate given by one leaseholder applies to the entire apartment. Any remaining occupant who wants to stay must apply and qualify for a new lease.

Your notice should include your planned move-out date and your forwarding address.

We will confirm receipt of your notice in writing within 2 business days.

## 5. Giving Less Than 60 Days' Notice

If you give less than 60 days' notice, you are responsible for rent through the 60th day after we receive your notice, or through your lease end date, whichever is later.

For example, if your lease ends on June 30 and we receive your notice on May 15, you are responsible for rent through July 14.

## 6. Cancelling a Notice to Vacate

A notice to vacate can be cancelled only with written approval from the leasing office.

We cannot approve a cancellation if the apartment has already been leased to a new resident.

## 7. If You Do Not Renew or Give Notice

If you neither sign a renewal lease nor give notice to vacate, your lease continues on a month-to-month basis.

Month-to-month rent is your current rent plus a month-to-month premium of $150 per month.

To end a month-to-month lease, you or we must give at least 30 days' written notice.

We may change month-to-month rent by giving you at least 30 days' written notice.

## 8. Early Termination

If you need to move out before your lease end date, you may end your lease early by completing all of the following steps.

1. Give at least 60 days' written notice of your early termination date, using the Notice to Vacate form.
2. Pay rent through your early termination date.
3. Pay an early termination fee equal to two months' rent, no later than 30 days before your early termination date.
4. Repay any rent concessions or move-in specials you received.

A resident who receives permanent change-of-station orders or deployment orders from the military may end the lease with 30 days' written notice and a copy of the orders, without paying the early termination fee.

If you move out early without following these steps, you remain responsible for rent through the end of the lease term or until the apartment is re-rented, whichever happens first, plus the cost of re-renting it.

## 9. Transferring to Another Apartment

If you would like to move to a different apartment at Larkfen Commons instead of moving out, ask the leasing office about available apartments before giving notice.

A unit transfer fee of $300 applies, and your security deposit moves with you to the new apartment after your current apartment is inspected.

## 10. Preparing to Move Out

After you give notice, follow the Move-Out Checklist and Deposit Return Guide for cleaning, key return and deposit information.

All keys, mailbox keys, amenity key fobs and parking hang tags must be returned to the leasing office by 12:00 PM (noon) on your move-out date.

Keys returned after 12:00 PM (noon) on the move-out date are charged a late key return fee of $50.

Questions about renewals or notices may be directed to the leasing office at (859) 555-0142 or office@ostervale.example.
