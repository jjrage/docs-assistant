# Rent Payment Guide

This guide explains when rent is due at Larkfen Commons, the ways you can pay, how autopay works, and what happens if a payment is late or returned. Your Residential Lease Agreement and the Fee Schedule are the official sources for every amount in this guide.

## 1. When Rent Is Due

Rent is due on the **1st day of every month**, including months when the 1st falls on a weekend or a holiday.

Rent received by 11:59 PM on the 5th day of the month is considered on time.

Your monthly statement in the resident portal also includes your utility service fee for water, sewer and trash, any pet rent, any parking or carport fees, and any other charges posted to your account. All of these are due with rent.

Rent is due as usual every month of the year. Rent is never waived or reduced unless a written agreement is signed by the Community Manager.

## 2. Ways to Pay

### Resident Portal

You can pay online any time in the resident portal at portal.ostervale.example.

E-check payments from a bank account (ACH) in the resident portal are free.

Debit card payments in the resident portal have a convenience fee of $3.95 per payment, charged by the payment processor.

Credit card payments in the resident portal have a convenience fee of 2.95% of the payment amount, charged by the payment processor.

Portal payments made by 11:59 PM Eastern Time on the 5th are considered on time, even if the bank takes a few days to process them.

### Autopay

Autopay is a free service that drafts your full balance due from your bank account (ACH) each month.

Autopay drafts on the 1st of the month by default, but you may choose any draft day from the 1st through the 5th.

To enroll in autopay, change your draft date, or cancel autopay, update your settings in the resident portal at least 3 business days before your next draft date.

Autopay drafts the full balance due on your account on the draft date, including any fees posted to your account.

Please make sure the account you use for autopay has enough funds. A returned autopay draft is treated like any other returned payment.

### Drop Box at the Leasing Office

You may pay by personal check, cashier's check or money order using the drop box beside the leasing office door at 4400 Hollin Mill Drive.

Make checks and money orders payable to "Larkfen Commons" and write your apartment number on the check.

Payments placed in the drop box by 11:59 PM on the 5th are considered on time.

### Cash

We do not accept cash payments at any time, in the office or in the drop box.

## 3. Late Charges

If rent is not paid in full by 11:59 PM on the 5th, an initial late charge of $75 is added to your account on the 6th.

A daily late charge of $10 is added each day starting on the 7th until rent is paid in full.

Total late charges for any one month will not exceed $150.

Late charges are added to your account and are due immediately.

### Late Charge Examples

The table below shows the total late charges for rent that is paid in full on different days of the month.

| Rent paid in full | Total late charges |
|---|---|
| By 11:59 PM on the 5th | No late charge |
| On the 6th | $75 initial late charge |
| On the 8th | $95, which is the $75 initial late charge plus $10 for the 7th and $10 for the 8th |
| On the 10th | $115, which is the $75 initial late charge plus $10 for each day from the 7th through the 10th |
| On the 14th or later | $150, the maximum total late charges for one month |

## 4. Returned Payments

A returned payment fee of $40 is charged for each payment that is returned or rejected by a bank for any reason.

If a returned payment makes your rent late, late charges are also added as if no payment had been made.

After two returned payments in any 12-month period, you must pay by certified funds only, meaning a cashier's check or money order, for the rest of your lease term. Portal and autopay payments are turned off for your account during this time.

## 5. Partial Payments and Payment Plans

Larkfen Commons does not accept partial rent payments unless you have a written payment plan signed by the Community Manager.

If you expect to have trouble paying rent, ask for a payment plan in writing by the 3rd of the month, by e-mail to billing@ostervale.example or through the resident portal.

Only one payment plan can be approved for a household in any 12-month period.

Late charges continue to apply to any unpaid balance unless the written payment plan says otherwise.

If you send a partial payment without an approved payment plan, it may be returned to you, and the full rent will be considered unpaid.

## 6. Prorated Rent

Rent for a partial month at move-in or move-out is prorated using a daily rent equal to the monthly rent divided by 30.

## 7. Protect Your Payment Information

Ostervale Property Management staff will never ask you to send bank account numbers, card numbers or passwords by e-mail, text message or phone.

Enter your payment information only in the resident portal at portal.ostervale.example.

If you receive a message asking for your bank details or offering a rent discount you did not expect, do not reply. Call the leasing office at (859) 555-0142 to check whether the message is real.

## 8. Receipts and Account History

Every payment made in the resident portal or through autopay generates an e-mail receipt.

Your full payment history and current balance are always available in the resident portal.

Drop box payments are posted to your account on the business day they are collected, and you can see them in the resident portal after posting.

## 9. Questions About Your Account

Questions about rent, charges or your account balance may be directed to resident billing at (859) 555-0127 or billing@ostervale.example.

Resident billing is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
