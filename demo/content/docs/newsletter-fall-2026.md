# Larkfen Commons Newsletter, Fall 2026

Happy fall, neighbors! The leaves along Hollin Mill Drive are turning, the pool has closed for the season, and the Larkfen Commons team is getting ready for a busy few months. Here is what is coming up.

## Fall Festival on October 24

Join us for our annual Fall Festival on Saturday, October 24, 2026, from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM at the clubhouse and picnic pavilion.

We will have hot cider, a chili tasting, pumpkin decorating for kids, and a photo booth with fall props. The event is free for residents and their guests, so bring the whole family.

If you would like to enter your chili in the tasting, sign up at the leasing office by Wednesday, October 21.

## Door Decorating Contest

Show off your Halloween spirit! Decorate your front door with an over-the-door hanger or a wreath, and send a photo to office@ostervale.example. Our team will judge every entry on Friday, October 30, 2026, and the winner receives a $50 gift card.

Please remember that nails, screws and tape are not allowed on apartment doors.

## Fall Food Drive

From November 2 through November 20, 2026, a collection bin will be in the clubhouse lobby for our fall food drive. Canned vegetables, soup, peanut butter, rice and pasta are especially needed. Every donation goes to a local food pantry before Thanksgiving.

## Getting Ready for Winter

Lexington winters can bring a hard freeze or two. To protect your apartment from frozen pipes, keep your heat set at 55°F or higher whenever outdoor temperatures are below freezing, even if you are traveling for the holidays. On the coldest nights, open the cabinet doors under any sink on an exterior wall.

If a pipe does freeze or burst, turn off the water at the main shutoff valve in your water heater closet and call the emergency line right away.

Holiday lights are welcome on balconies and patios starting November 15.
