# Trash, Recycling and Bulk Item Pickup

Keeping Larkfen Commons clean is a shared job. This guide explains where trash and recycling go, when they are collected, and how to get rid of furniture and other large items. Trash service is included in your monthly utility service fee.

## 1. Trash Enclosures

There is one trash enclosure beside each building: Building A, Building B and Building C.

Each enclosure has dumpsters for household trash and blue containers for recycling.

You may take trash to the enclosures only between 7:00 AM and 10:00 PM, out of respect for neighbors during quiet hours.

## 2. Household Trash

All household trash must be placed in tied plastic bags.

Bags must be placed inside a dumpster, not beside it or on top of a closed lid. If a dumpster is full, please use the next enclosure.

Trash bags may not be left outside apartment doors, in hallways, on landings, or on balconies or patios, even for a short time.

Trash left outside a dumpster, in a hallway or on a balcony results in a charge of $50 per bag or item.

Used cat litter must be bagged and tied before it goes in a dumpster.

## 3. Collection Days

Dumpsters are emptied every Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

Recycling containers are emptied every Tuesday.

If a collection day falls on a holiday when the leasing office is closed, collection takes place the next day.

## 4. Recycling

Please rinse containers and place recycling loose in the blue containers. Do not put recycling in plastic bags.

| Accepted in the blue containers | Not accepted |
|---|---|
| Flattened cardboard boxes and paperboard, such as cereal boxes | Plastic bags, plastic wrap and bubble wrap |
| Newspaper, magazines, mail and office paper | Foam cups, foam trays and foam packing material |
| Plastic bottles and jugs marked #1 or #2 | Food waste and liquids |
| Aluminum and steel cans | Greasy pizza boxes and paper plates |
| Glass bottles and jars | Hoses, extension cords and string lights |
| Clean aluminum foil and trays | Electronics, batteries and light bulbs |

All cardboard boxes must be flattened before they go in a recycling container. Boxes that are not flattened fill the containers quickly and may be left behind by the hauler.

If the recycling containers are full, please hold your recycling until after the Tuesday collection. Do not leave recycling on the ground.

## 5. Bulk Item Pickup

Bulk items include furniture, mattresses, box springs, rugs, large toys and other household items that do not fit in a dumpster.

Bulk item pickup takes place on the 2nd and 4th Thursday of each month.

To schedule a bulk item pickup, submit a request in the resident portal or at the leasing office by 5:00 PM on the Tuesday before the pickup date.

Each request may include up to 4 items, and the fee is $30 per item. The fee is posted to your resident account.

Place your items beside your building's trash enclosure no earlier than 6:00 PM on Wednesday and no later than 7:00 AM on the Thursday of the pickup.

Mattresses and box springs must be completely wrapped in plastic before they are set out for pickup.

Furniture, mattresses or other bulk items left at an enclosure without a scheduled pickup result in a charge of $150 per item.

## 6. Items We Cannot Take

The following items are not accepted in the dumpsters, the recycling containers or the bulk item pickup: tires, paint, motor oil, chemicals, pool chemicals, batteries, propane tanks, and electronics such as televisions and computers.

Please take these items to a household hazardous waste or electronics drop-off site run by the city or county.

## 7. Moving Boxes

Flatten all moving boxes and place them in the blue recycling containers.

If you have a large number of boxes, spread them across more than one enclosure or hold some until after the next Tuesday collection.

## 8. Holiday Trees

Real holiday trees may be left beside any trash enclosure from December 26 through January 15 at no charge.

All lights, ornaments, tinsel and stands must be removed before the tree is set out.

Artificial trees are treated as bulk items and must be scheduled for bulk item pickup.

## 9. Tips for Keeping the Enclosures Clean

Close the dumpster lid after you use it so that animals and rain stay out.

Break down boxes inside your apartment before you carry them to the enclosure. It is faster and keeps the enclosure tidy.

Double-bag wet or heavy trash so the bag does not tear on the way to the dumpster.

If you see a broken dumpster lid, an overflowing container, or items left outside an enclosure, let the leasing office know at (859) 555-0142 so our team can take care of it.

Please do not climb into dumpsters or remove items from them, for your own safety.

## 10. Questions

Questions about trash, recycling or bulk item pickup may be directed to the leasing office at (859) 555-0142 or office@ostervale.example.
