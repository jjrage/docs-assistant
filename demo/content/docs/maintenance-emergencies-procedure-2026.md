# Maintenance Requests and Emergencies Procedure

This procedure explains how Larkfen Commons residents request repairs, how quickly the Ostervale Property Management maintenance team responds, and what to do in an emergency. Please keep it somewhere easy to find, such as inside a kitchen cabinet door.

## 1. How to Submit a Maintenance Request

The fastest way to request a repair is through the resident portal at portal.ostervale.example, which accepts requests 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

When you submit a request in the resident portal, describe the problem, the room where it is located, and when it started. You may attach up to five photos.

During office hours, you may also call the leasing office at (859) 555-0142 and a staff member will enter the request for you.

For emergencies after office hours, on weekends or on holidays, call the 24-hour emergency maintenance line at **(859) 555-0199**.

Do not use the emergency line for routine requests. Routine requests made to the emergency line will be entered in the system and handled on the next business day.

Each request receives a work order number. You can follow the status of your request in the resident portal.

## 2. Maintenance Hours

The maintenance team's regular hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

An on-call technician is available 24 hours a day, every day of the year, for emergencies only.

## 3. Response Times by Priority

Every request is assigned one of four priority levels.

| Priority | Examples | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency | Active water leak, sewage backup, no heat in cold weather, fire damage | The on-call technician calls you back within 30 minutes and arrives within 2 hours, 24 hours a day. |
| Urgent | No hot water, refrigerator not cooling, air conditioning not working when the outdoor temperature is below 90°F | Work begins within 24 hours of the request. |
| Routine | Dripping faucet, running toilet when another toilet works, jammed garbage disposal, dishwasher not draining | Work is completed within 3 business days of the request. |
| Cosmetic or scheduled | Paint touch-up, loose cabinet door, broken blind slat, sticking closet door | Work is scheduled within 10 business days of the request. |

If a repair requires parts that must be ordered, the technician will tell you the expected date, and the status will be updated in the resident portal.

## 4. What Counts as an Emergency

An emergency is a problem that threatens health or safety or could cause serious damage to the building if it is not fixed right away.

The following situations are maintenance emergencies:

- Fire or smoke in your apartment or building. Call 911 first.
- An active water leak or flooding that you cannot stop.
- A sewage backup into a sink, tub, shower or toilet.
- No heat when the outdoor temperature is below 55°F.
- No air conditioning when the outdoor temperature is 90°F or higher.
- A total loss of electricity in your apartment after you have checked the breaker panel.
- The only toilet in your apartment does not work.
- An exterior door that will not lock, or a broken window on the ground floor.
- A lockout outside the hours of 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on weekdays.

If the power is out across the whole community or neighborhood, contact the local electric utility, because the outage is not something our team can repair.

All apartments at Larkfen Commons are all-electric. If you ever smell gas anywhere on the property, leave the area immediately and call 911.

## 5. What to Do in a Water Leak

1. Turn off the water at the shutoff valve for the leaking fixture. Shutoff valves are located under sinks and behind toilets.
2. If you cannot find the fixture valve or the leak is in a wall or ceiling, turn off the main water shutoff valve, which is located inside the water heater closet.
3. If water is near electrical outlets, light fixtures or appliances, turn off the breaker for that area at the breaker panel and stay out of standing water.
4. Call the 24-hour emergency maintenance line at (859) 555-0199.
5. Move belongings away from the water and use towels to soak up as much water as you safely can.
6. Take photos of the damage for your renter's insurance claim.
7. Submit a work order in the resident portal with the details and photos so there is a written record.

If water is coming into your apartment from the apartment above, call the emergency line right away. Our team will contact the resident upstairs.

## 6. What to Do in a Fire

1. Leave the apartment immediately and close the door behind you.
2. Pull the nearest fire alarm pull station on your way out of the building.
3. Call 911 once you are safely outside.
4. Do not go back inside until the fire department says it is safe.
5. After the fire department has responded, call the emergency maintenance line at (859) 555-0199.

A portable fire extinguisher is located under your kitchen sink. Use it only on a small fire and only if you can do so safely. If you use the extinguisher, report it in the resident portal and we will replace it at no charge.

## 7. What to Do If You Are Locked Out

From Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, come to the leasing office with a photo ID and a staff member will unlock your door. The lockout fee during these hours is $25.

At all other times, including evenings, weekends and holidays, call the 24-hour emergency maintenance line at (859) 555-0199. The after-hours lockout fee is $75.

We will only unlock a door for a leaseholder or an approved adult occupant whose photo ID matches our records.

Lockout fees are posted to your resident account and are due with your next rent payment.

## 8. Permission to Enter

When you submit a maintenance request, you give our team permission to enter your apartment to complete the work between 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM on business days, even if you are not home.

If you would like us to call before entering, write "call before entry" in the request.

Pets must be secured in a crate or a closed room while technicians are working. If a pet is loose, the technician will leave and reschedule the request.

After entering, the technician leaves a service notice describing the work that was done.

## 9. Your Maintenance Responsibilities

Residents are responsible for replacing light bulbs. Standard bulbs are installed in every fixture at move-in.

Test your smoke detectors at least once a month and replace their batteries when needed.

Do not pour grease, oil or coffee grounds into sinks or the garbage disposal.

Keep the heat set at 55°F or higher whenever outdoor temperatures are below freezing, including when you are away, to prevent frozen pipes.

During hard freezes, open the cabinet doors under sinks that are on exterior walls so warm air can reach the pipes.

Our maintenance team replaces heating and air conditioning filters every 3 months, in January, April, July and October, at no charge.

### Seasonal Maintenance Calendar

| Month | What happens |
|---|---|
| January, April, July and October | Our maintenance team replaces the heating and air conditioning filter in every apartment. |
| March | Our team tests the heating and air conditioning system in each building before the cooling season. |
| May | The pool is prepared for opening on the Saturday of Memorial Day weekend. |
| September | The pool is closed and winterized after Labor Day. |
| November | Residents should begin keeping the heat at 55°F or higher whenever outdoor temperatures are below freezing. |

You will receive a notice at least 24 hours before any visit that requires entry into your apartment.

## 10. Charges for Resident-Caused Damage

Repairs of damage caused by residents, occupants, guests or pets are charged at $65 per hour of labor, with a one-hour minimum, plus the cost of materials.

Examples include items flushed down a toilet that cause a clog, broken blinds, and holes in walls or doors.

## 11. If a Request Is Not Completed on Time

If your request has not been completed within the response time for its priority, call the leasing office at (859) 555-0142.

If the problem is still not resolved, you may contact Maintenance Supervisor Luis Ortega or Community Manager Maria Delgado through the leasing office.

If you are still not satisfied, contact the Ostervale Property Management corporate office at (859) 555-0110.
