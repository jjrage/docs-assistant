# Community Rules

These Community Rules apply to all residents, occupants and guests at Larkfen Commons, managed by Ostervale Property Management. They are part of your Residential Lease Agreement. Our goal is simple: a clean, safe and quiet place to live for all 180 households in Buildings A, B and C.

## 1. Quiet Hours and Noise

Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM on Sunday through Thursday nights.

Quiet hours are 11:00 PM to 9:00 AM on Friday and Saturday nights.

During quiet hours, keep music, televisions, gaming and conversations at a level that cannot be heard outside your apartment.

At all times, noise should not disturb your neighbors. This includes loud music, slamming doors, heavy footsteps on upper floors, and dogs barking for long periods.

Washers, dryers, vacuums and dishwashers should not be run during quiet hours if they can be heard in a neighboring apartment.

If a neighbor is too loud during office hours, call the leasing office at (859) 555-0142.

After office hours, report an ongoing noise disturbance to the 24-hour emergency maintenance line at (859) 555-0199. The call is logged and the on-call manager follows up.

If you believe anyone is in danger, call 911 first.

We do not share the name of the resident who made a noise complaint.

## 2. Common Areas

Hallways, stairways, breezeways and landings must be kept clear of personal belongings at all times, because they are fire exits.

You may place one doormat, no larger than 2 by 3 feet, outside your apartment door.

Shoes, strollers, toys, furniture and trash bags may not be left in hallways or on landings.

Trash bags left in hallways or common areas result in a charge of $50 per bag.

Exterior building doors must not be propped open.

Children under 10 must be supervised by an adult when playing in common areas. Children may not play in the parking lot or driveways.

Skateboarding, scooter riding and roller skating are not allowed in hallways, breezeways or the clubhouse.

## 3. Balconies and Patios

Balconies and patios may be used only for outdoor furniture and potted plants. Plant pots must sit on saucers so water does not drip onto the balcony below.

Laundry, towels, rugs and blankets may not be hung over balcony or patio railings.

Indoor furniture, appliances, boxes and trash may not be kept on balconies or patios.

Holiday lights may be displayed on balconies and patios from November 15 through January 15.

Feeding birds, squirrels or other wildlife is not allowed, and bird feeders may not be hung on balconies or patios.

Do not shake rugs or sweep debris over balcony railings.

## 4. Grills and Open Flames

Charcoal grills, propane grills and all other open-flame cooking devices are not allowed on balconies or patios.

Charcoal and propane grills may not be used within 10 feet of any building.

Electric grills are allowed on balconies and patios.

The picnic pavilion next to the pool has two built-in propane grills that residents may use from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on a first-come, first-served basis. The pavilion grills cannot be reserved.

Please clean the pavilion grill after each use and turn off the gas valve.

Candles and other open flames must never be left unattended in your apartment.

Fireworks of any kind are not allowed anywhere at Larkfen Commons.

## 5. Smoke-Free Community

Larkfen Commons is a smoke-free community.

Smoking and vaping, including cigarettes, cigars, pipes, e-cigarettes and marijuana, are not allowed inside apartments, on balconies or patios, in common areas, or within 25 feet of any building.

The designated smoking area is the covered gazebo at the north end of the parking lot near Building C.

Please use the ash receptacle at the gazebo and do not throw cigarette butts on the ground.

A smoking violation results in a charge of $250 per occurrence, plus the cost of removing smoke odor and residue from the apartment.

## 6. Guests

Residents are responsible for the behavior of their guests and for any damage their guests cause.

A guest may stay in your apartment for no more than 14 consecutive days, and no more than 30 days in total in any 12-month period, without written approval from the leasing office.

Guests who stay longer must apply to be added to the lease as an occupant or leaseholder.

Guests must park in spaces marked "VISITOR" and follow the guest parking rules in the Parking Policy.

Guests using the pool or fitness center must be accompanied by a resident at all times, as described in the Amenities Rules.

## 7. Windows, Doors and Appearance

Window blinds provided by the community must remain in place. Aluminum foil, sheets, flags and signs may not be placed in windows.

Seasonal wreaths and decorations may be hung on your front door with an over-the-door hanger. Do not put nails, screws or tape on apartment doors.

Exterior doors and windows must be kept closed when the heat or air conditioning is running.

## 8. Pets and Animals

All pets must be registered with the leasing office and follow the current Pet Policy.

Dogs must be on a leash no longer than 6 feet in all common areas.

Pet waste must be picked up immediately and placed in a pet waste station or dumpster.

## 9. Reporting Problems and Violations

Report maintenance problems through the resident portal, or call (859) 555-0199 for emergencies after hours.

Report rule violations to the leasing office in person, by phone at (859) 555-0142, or by e-mail at office@ostervale.example.

## 10. Rule Violations and Fines

Unless a different charge is listed in these rules or in the Fee Schedule, violations of the Community Rules are handled as follows.

The first violation results in a written warning.

The second violation results in a $50 fine.

The third and each later violation within 12 months results in a $100 fine.

Repeated or serious violations may also be treated as a default under the Residential Lease Agreement.

Fines are posted to your resident account and are due with your next rent payment.

## 11. Laundry and Appliances

Every apartment has a full-size washer and dryer. Please do not leave the washer or dryer running while no one is home.

Clean the dryer lint trap after every load. A full lint trap makes the dryer work harder and can be a fire hazard.

If you notice water under the washer or a burning smell from the dryer, stop using the appliance and submit a maintenance request right away.

Do not run the dishwasher, washer or dryer during quiet hours if the noise can be heard in a neighboring apartment.

## 12. Holiday and Seasonal Decorations

Holiday lights may be displayed on balconies and patios from November 15 through January 15. Please remove them by January 15.

Only lights rated for outdoor use may be used outside, and cords may not run through windows or under doors.

Decorations in hallways and on landings must not block walkways, door handles or the view of apartment numbers.

Inflatable decorations, real candles and string lights may not be used in hallways, breezeways or stairways.
