# Renter's Insurance Requirement

Every household at Larkfen Commons must carry renter's insurance. This document explains what coverage is required, how to show proof, and what happens if a policy lapses. It is part of your Residential Lease Agreement.

## 1. Why Renter's Insurance Is Required

Ostervale Property Management insures the buildings at Larkfen Commons, but that insurance does not cover your personal belongings or your personal liability.

If a fire, a water leak, or an accident that starts in your apartment damages a neighbor's apartment or the building, you may be responsible for the cost. Personal liability coverage protects you in that situation.

## 2. Required Coverage

You must carry renter's insurance for the entire lease term, including any renewal term and any month-to-month period.

Your policy must include at least $100,000 of personal liability coverage per occurrence.

The policy must cover every leaseholder on the lease. Roommates may share one policy that names all of them, or each leaseholder may have a separate policy.

You may buy your policy from any licensed insurance provider of your choice.

## 3. Interested Party

Your policy must list Ostervale Property Management as an interested party, using this address: 250 Corvane Street, Suite 400, Lexington, KY 40507.

Listing us as an interested party means your insurance company will notify us if your policy is cancelled or not renewed. It does not give us any rights to your coverage.

Ostervale Property Management does not need to be listed as an additional insured.

## 4. Recommended Coverage

Personal property coverage is not required, but we strongly recommend it. It helps pay to replace your belongings after a covered loss, such as a fire or theft.

We also recommend coverage for additional living expenses, which helps pay for a hotel or other housing if your apartment cannot be lived in after a covered loss.

Ask your insurance agent whether your policy covers water backup from drains, which some basic policies do not include.

## 5. Proof of Insurance

You must provide proof of insurance before we release keys to you at move-in.

You must also provide proof of insurance at each lease renewal and whenever you change insurance companies.

Proof of insurance is the declarations page of your policy, showing the policy number, the names of the insured leaseholders, the policy dates, the liability limit, and Ostervale Property Management as an interested party.

Upload your declarations page in the resident portal or send it by e-mail to insurance@ostervale.example.

## 6. If Your Policy Lapses or Is Cancelled

If your insurance company tells us your policy has lapsed or been cancelled, we will notify you in writing.

You then have 10 days to provide proof of a new policy that meets the requirements in Section 2.

If proof is not received within 10 days, a renter's insurance lapse fee of $25 per month is charged until proof is received.

Not carrying renter's insurance is a violation of your Residential Lease Agreement, even if you pay the insurance lapse fee.

Paying the insurance lapse fee does not give you any insurance coverage.

## 7. Making a Claim

If you have a loss, contact your insurance company as soon as possible to start a claim.

For water leaks and other damage in your apartment, also submit a maintenance request in the resident portal, or call the 24-hour emergency maintenance line at (859) 555-0199 if it is an emergency.

Take dated photos of the damage before you clean up, and keep receipts for any emergency expenses.

The leasing office can give your insurance company a copy of the work order for the repair.

## 8. Common Questions

### My roommate has a policy. Am I covered?

Only if your name is listed on that policy as an insured person. If it is not, you need your own policy or must be added to your roommate's policy.

### Does my policy need to start on my move-in date?

Yes. The policy must be in effect on the day you pick up your keys, and the declarations page must show a start date no later than your move-in date.

### I renewed my lease. Do I need to send proof again?

Yes. Upload the current declarations page for every renewal term, even if you have kept the same insurance company.

### Does the insurance lapse fee buy me insurance?

No. The $25 monthly insurance lapse fee does not provide any insurance coverage for you, your belongings or your liability.

### Does the community's insurance pay for my things after a leak from upstairs?

No. Ostervale Property Management's property insurance covers the buildings only. Your own renter's insurance is what pays for your belongings.
