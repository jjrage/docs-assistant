# Community Contacts Directory

This directory lists the phone numbers, e-mail addresses and office hours for Larkfen Commons and Ostervale Property Management. Keep a copy on your refrigerator or save the numbers in your phone.

## 1. In an Emergency

For fire, a medical emergency, a crime in progress, or any threat to someone's safety, call 911 first.

After calling 911, call the 24-hour emergency maintenance line at (859) 555-0199 if the emergency involves your apartment or building.

If you smell gas anywhere on the property, leave the area and call 911.

## 7. Which Contact to Use

Use the resident portal for non-emergency maintenance requests, rent payments, renewal offers and notices to vacate.

Call the leasing office during office hours for general questions, parking permits, guest parking passes, clubhouse reservations, and lockouts on weekdays between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM.

Call the 24-hour emergency maintenance line after hours for maintenance emergencies, lockouts, and ongoing noise disturbances.

The lockout fee is $25 from Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and $75 at all other times.

Contact resident billing with questions about rent, charges, payment plans or your deposit statement.

Contact the corporate office only if a problem has not been resolved by the community staff.

## 8. After-Hours Calls

When you call the 24-hour emergency maintenance line, an answering service takes your name, apartment number, phone number and a description of the problem.

For emergencies, the on-call technician calls you back within 30 minutes and arrives within 2 hours.

Non-emergency calls to the emergency line are logged and handled on the next business day.

## 9. Before You Call

Have your name, building, apartment number and a phone number where you can be reached ready before you call.

For maintenance problems, describe what is wrong, where it is, and when it started, and mention any pets in the apartment.

For billing questions, have your most recent statement from the resident portal open so staff can find the charge quickly.

If you are reporting a noise disturbance after hours, give the building and apartment number the noise is coming from, if you know it.

## 10. Utilities

Electric service is provided by the local electric utility. Contact the utility directly to start or stop service, report an area-wide outage, or ask about your electric bill.

Water, sewer and trash service are billed by Larkfen Commons as a monthly utility service fee with your rent.
