# Move-In Checklist and Condition Report Instructions

Welcome to Larkfen Commons! This checklist walks you through everything you need to do before, during and right after your move-in. Following it closely will make move-in day smooth and will protect your security deposit when you eventually move out.

## 1. Before Move-In Day

- Sign your Residential Lease Agreement and any addenda, such as a pet addendum or a carport addendum.
- Pay all move-in funds no later than 5:00 PM on the business day before your move-in date.
- Move-in funds include your prorated first month's rent, the $200 administrative fee, your security deposit and any pet deposit.
- Move-in funds must be paid through the resident portal or by certified funds (a cashier's check or money order). Personal checks are not accepted for move-in funds.
- Buy a renter's insurance policy with at least $100,000 of personal liability coverage, list Ostervale Property Management as an interested party, and upload the declarations page in the resident portal.
- Open an electric service account in your name with the local electric utility, starting on your move-in date, and write down the confirmation number.
- Register each pet with the leasing office before the pet arrives, as required by the Pet Policy.
- Give the leasing office the make, model, color and license plate number of each vehicle you will park at the community.

## 2. Key Pickup

Keys are picked up at the leasing office in the clubhouse at 4400 Hollin Mill Drive.

Key pickup hours are Monday through Friday from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM and Saturday from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

We do not schedule move-ins on Sundays or on days when the leasing office is closed for a holiday.

We cannot release keys until all move-in funds are paid, proof of renter's insurance is on file, and you have given us the confirmation number for your electric service.

Every adult leaseholder must bring a photo ID to key pickup.

## 3. What You Will Receive

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Apartment keys | Two apartment keys per apartment |
| Mailbox key | One mailbox key per apartment |
| Amenity key fob | One amenity key fob for each adult leaseholder |
| Parking hang tag | One hang tag for each registered vehicle, up to two per apartment |
| Move-In Condition Report | One blank report form, also available in the resident portal |

Please keep track of every key, fob and hang tag. All of them must be returned at move-out, and lost items are charged as listed in the Fee Schedule.

## 4. Moving Day Rules

Moving hours are 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, every day of the week.

Moving trucks may park in visitor spaces for up to 4 hours between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM. Trucks must never block fire lanes, trash enclosures or other vehicles.

Do not prop open exterior building doors and leave them unattended.

Flatten moving boxes and place them in the blue recycling containers in your building's trash enclosure. Do not leave boxes in hallways or beside the dumpsters.

## 5. The Move-In Condition Report

The Move-In Condition Report is your written record of the condition of the apartment on the day you receive your keys. We use it at move-out to decide which damage was already there and which damage happened during your residency.

You must complete the Move-In Condition Report and return it within **5 calendar days** after you pick up your keys.

You may return the report by uploading it in the resident portal or by bringing the signed paper form to the leasing office.

Any item that is not noted on the report will be considered to have been in good condition at move-in.

### How to Fill Out the Report

1. Walk through every room, including closets, the water heater closet, and the balcony or patio.
2. For each item on the form, mark it as Good, Fair or Damaged.
3. Describe every scratch, stain, chip, hole or dent, including its size and location. For example, write "two-inch scratch on the bedroom door, about waist height."
4. Take dated photos of anything you note, and keep copies for yourself. You may upload photos together with the report.
5. Open and close every window, door, cabinet and drawer, and note anything that sticks or does not latch.
6. Run every faucet, flush every toilet, and check under sinks for drips.
7. Test every appliance, including the refrigerator, range, oven, dishwasher, microwave, garbage disposal, washer and dryer.
8. Sign and date the report. Every adult leaseholder should sign it.

### Rooms and Items on the Report

| Area | Items to check |
|---|---|
| Entry and living room | Front door and lock, walls, ceiling, flooring, light fixtures, outlets, windows and blinds |
| Kitchen | Cabinets, countertops, sink and faucet, refrigerator, range and oven, dishwasher, microwave, garbage disposal |
| Bedrooms | Doors, closet doors, walls, carpet, windows and blinds, ceiling fan or light |
| Bathrooms | Toilet, tub or shower, sink, mirror, vanity, tile and caulking, exhaust fan |
| Laundry | Washer, dryer, dryer vent connection |
| Balcony or patio | Railing, flooring, door and door track, exterior light |
| Safety | Smoke detectors and fire extinguisher under the kitchen sink |

### The Condition Report Is Not a Repair Request

The Move-In Condition Report records the condition of the apartment, but it does not create a work order.

If something needs to be repaired, such as a dripping faucet or an appliance that does not work, submit a separate maintenance request in the resident portal.

## 6. Smoke Detectors

A staff member will test the smoke detectors with you during your move-in walkthrough.

After move-in, test your smoke detectors at least once a month and replace their batteries when needed.

## 7. Your First Week

- Return the signed Move-In Condition Report within 5 calendar days.
- Log in to the resident portal and set up your rent payment method or autopay.
- Hang your parking hang tag on your rearview mirror before parking overnight.
- Locate the main water shutoff valve inside your water heater closet and the breaker panel.
- Save the 24-hour emergency maintenance line, (859) 555-0199, in your phone.
- Read the Community Rules, including quiet hours, and the Trash, Recycling and Bulk Item Pickup guide.

Questions about move-in may be directed to the leasing office at (859) 555-0142 or office@ostervale.example.
