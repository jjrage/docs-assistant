# Fee Schedule

This Fee Schedule lists every fee, deposit and charge that may apply to residents of Larkfen Commons, managed by Ostervale Property Management. It is effective March 1, 2026 and is part of your Residential Lease Agreement. Fees are posted to your resident account and, unless stated otherwise, are due with your next rent payment.

## 1. Application and Move-In

| Fee | Amount | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Application fee | $50 per applicant | A non-refundable $50 fee charged to each applicant age 18 or older when the application is submitted. |
| Administrative fee | $200 per apartment | A non-refundable $200 fee charged once per apartment at lease signing. |
| Security deposit | $500 standard | The standard deposit is $500. Applicants approved with conditions pay a security deposit equal to one full month's rent. |

## 2. Rent and Payments

| Fee | Amount | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Initial late charge | $75 | A $75 charge added on the 6th of the month if rent is not paid in full by 11:59 PM on the 5th. |
| Daily late charge | $10 per day | Added each day starting on the 7th until rent is paid in full. Total late charges never exceed $150 per month. |
| Returned payment fee | $40 per payment | A $40 fee charged for each payment returned or rejected by a bank for any reason. |
| Debit card payment | $3.95 per payment | A $3.95 convenience fee charged by the payment processor for each debit card payment in the resident portal. |
| Credit card payment | 2.95% of the payment | A convenience fee of 2.95% of the payment, charged by the payment processor for credit card payments in the resident portal. |
| E-check and autopay | No charge | Bank account (ACH) payments in the resident portal, including autopay, are free. |

## 3. Keys, Locks and Access

| Fee | Amount | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Weekday daytime lockout | $25 | A $25 fee for lockout service from Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, at the leasing office. |
| After-hours lockout | $75 | A $75 fee for lockout service at any other time, requested through the emergency line at (859) 555-0199. |
| Apartment key replacement | $25 per key | A $25 charge for each lost apartment key or each apartment key not returned at move-out. |
| Mailbox key replacement | $15 per key | A $15 charge for each lost mailbox key or each mailbox key not returned at move-out. |
| Amenity key fob replacement | $30 per fob | A $30 charge for each lost amenity key fob or each fob not returned at move-out. |
| Rekey at resident request | $60 | A $60 fee when a resident asks our maintenance team to rekey the apartment locks. |

## 4. Pet Fees

Pet fees follow the Pet Policy effective March 1, 2026.

| Fee | Amount | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Pet deposit | $400 per pet | A refundable deposit of $400 paid when each pet is registered, held separately from the security deposit. |
| Pet rent | $35 per pet per month | Monthly pet rent of $35 for each registered pet, due with rent on the 1st of each month. |
| Unauthorized pet fee | $250 per animal | A $250 fee for each unregistered animal found in an apartment, plus pet rent back to the date it arrived. |
| Pet waste violation | $50 per occurrence | A $50 charge each time a resident fails to pick up after a pet anywhere on the property. |

## 5. Parking

| Fee | Amount | Details |
|---|---|---|
| First vehicle permit | Included | The parking permit for the first registered vehicle is included in rent. |
| Second vehicle permit | $20 per month | A permit for a second registered vehicle costs $20 per month. Maximum two permits per apartment. |
| Reserved covered carport | $45 per month | Monthly rent of $45 for one of the 30 reserved covered carports, assigned from a waitlist. |
| Replacement hang tag | $25 | A $25 charge for each lost or damaged parking hang tag, or each hang tag not returned at move-out. |
| Guest parking pass | No charge | Temporary guest passes from the leasing office are free and valid for up to 7 days. |

Towing and impound fees are set by and paid directly to the contracted towing company, not to Ostervale Property Management.

## 6. Utilities

| Fee | Amount | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Utility service fee, one bedroom | $45 per month | A monthly charge of $45 for water, sewer and trash service in a one-bedroom apartment. |
| Utility service fee, two bedrooms | $60 per month | A monthly charge of $60 for water, sewer and trash service in a two-bedroom apartment. |
| Utility service fee, three bedrooms | $75 per month | A monthly charge of $75 for water, sewer and trash service in a three-bedroom apartment. |
| Electric service lapse fee | $50 per occurrence | A $50 fee when electric service in the resident's name lapses, plus the cost of electricity used during the lapse. |

## 7. Amenities

| Fee | Amount | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Clubhouse lounge reservation fee | $50 per reservation | The $50 fee is non-refundable unless the reservation is cancelled at least 72 hours before the start time. |
| Clubhouse lounge damage deposit | $100 per reservation | The $100 deposit is refundable and is returned within 10 business days after the lounge passes inspection. |
| Amenity key fob, first issue | No charge | One amenity key fob is issued free of charge to each adult leaseholder at move-in. |

## 8. Trash and Community Rules

| Fee | Amount | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Bulk item pickup | $30 per item | Scheduled bulk item pickup costs $30 per item, up to 4 items per request, on the 2nd and 4th Thursday of each month. |
| Trash left outside a dumpster | $50 per bag or item | A $50 charge for each bag or item left beside a dumpster, outside a trash enclosure, in a hallway or on a balcony. |
| Bulk item left without pickup | $150 per item | A $150 charge for each piece of furniture, mattress or other bulk item left at an enclosure without a scheduled pickup. |
| Community rule violation | Warning, then $50, then $100 | A written warning for the first violation, $50 for the second, and $100 for the third and each later violation within 12 months. |
| Smoking violation | $250 per occurrence | A $250 charge for each smoking or vaping violation, plus the cost of removing smoke odor and residue. |
| Disabling a smoke detector | $100 per occurrence | A $100 charge each time a smoke detector is disconnected, removed or has its batteries removed. |

## 9. Lease Changes, Renewal and Move-Out

| Fee | Amount | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Lease change fee | $150 | A $150 fee to add or remove a leaseholder during the lease term. New adult applicants also pay the $50 application fee. |
| Unit transfer fee | $300 | A $300 fee when a resident transfers to a different apartment at Larkfen Commons. |
| Month-to-month premium | $150 per month | A $150 monthly premium added to current rent when a lease continues month to month without a renewal. |
| Early termination fee | Two months' rent | A fee equal to two months' rent, due no later than 30 days before the early termination date, with 60 days' written notice. |
| Late key return fee | $50 | A $50 fee when keys are returned after 12:00 PM (noon) on the move-out date. |
| Holdover rent | 150% of daily rent | Charged at 150% of the daily rent for each day a resident stays in the apartment after the lease ends without written consent. |

Cleaning and damage charges at move-out are listed in the Move-Out Checklist.

## 10. Insurance and Repairs

| Fee | Amount | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Renter's insurance lapse fee | $25 per month | A $25 monthly fee charged after a 10-day grace period until proof of a new renter's insurance policy is received. |
| Maintenance labor for resident-caused damage | $65 per hour | Labor of $65 per hour, with a one-hour minimum, plus materials, for repairs of damage caused by residents, occupants, guests or pets. |

## 11. Changes to This Fee Schedule

We may change this Fee Schedule by giving residents at least 30 days' written notice.

Questions about any charge on your account may be directed to resident billing at (859) 555-0127 or billing@ostervale.example, Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
