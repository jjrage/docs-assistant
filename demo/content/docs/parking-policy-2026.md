# Parking Policy

This Parking Policy applies to all residents and guests of Larkfen Commons, managed by Ostervale Property Management. It is part of your Residential Lease Agreement. Parking at the community is limited, so please follow these rules so that everyone can find a space.

## 1. Resident Parking

Open parking spaces at Larkfen Commons are unassigned and are available on a first-come, first-served basis.

Every resident vehicle parked at the community must display a valid Larkfen Commons parking hang tag on the rearview mirror.

Each apartment may register up to two vehicles.

The parking permit for the first vehicle is included in your rent at no extra charge.

A parking permit for a second vehicle costs $20 per month, billed with rent.

To register a vehicle, bring your vehicle registration and proof of auto insurance to the leasing office. The vehicle must be registered to a leaseholder or an approved occupant.

If you replace your vehicle, register the new vehicle with the leasing office within 7 days.

A replacement hang tag for a lost or damaged permit costs $25.

Hang tags must be returned to the leasing office at move-out. An unreturned hang tag is charged $25.

## 2. Reserved Covered Carports

Larkfen Commons has 30 reserved covered carports, which rent for $45 per month.

Carports are assigned by the leasing office from a waitlist in the order requests are received.

Each carport is numbered and may be used only by the resident it is assigned to. Carport renters sign a carport addendum.

A carport assignment may be ended by either party with 30 days' written notice.

## 3. Guest Parking

Guests must park in the spaces marked "VISITOR" near the clubhouse and near each building.

A guest vehicle may park in a visitor space for up to 48 hours without a pass.

For stays longer than 48 hours, the resident must get a temporary guest parking pass from the leasing office. Guest passes are free and are valid for up to 7 days at a time.

Residents may not park their own vehicles in visitor spaces at any time.

Guest stays are also limited by the Community Rules, which allow a guest to stay no more than 14 consecutive days without written approval.

## 4. Moving Trucks and Service Vehicles

Moving trucks may park in visitor spaces for up to 4 hours between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM.

Moving trucks and service vehicles must never block fire lanes, trash enclosures or other vehicles.

## 5. Vehicles and Uses Not Allowed

Boats, trailers, campers, recreational vehicles and commercial vehicles over 10,000 pounds may not be parked anywhere at Larkfen Commons.

Every vehicle must be operable, have current registration, and have inflated tires.

Vehicle repairs and maintenance are not allowed on the property, except jump-starting a battery or changing a flat tire.

Oil changes, fluid changes and bodywork are not allowed anywhere on the property. Any fluid leak must be cleaned up by the vehicle owner.

Motorcycles must display a valid permit and must park in a regular parking space. Only one motorcycle may park in a space.

The speed limit everywhere in the community is 10 MPH.

## 6. Towing Without Warning

The following vehicles may be towed immediately, at any time, without a warning notice:

- Vehicles parked in a fire lane.
- Vehicles parked in an accessible parking space without a valid accessible parking placard or plate.
- Vehicles blocking a trash enclosure, a dumpster or another vehicle.
- Vehicles parked on grass, sidewalks or landscaped areas.
- Vehicles parked in another resident's reserved carport.

## 7. Towing After a Warning Notice

The following vehicles receive a warning notice on the driver's side window and may be towed 48 hours later if the problem is not corrected:

- Vehicles in resident parking without a valid hang tag.
- Vehicles with expired registration.
- Inoperable vehicles, including vehicles with flat tires.
- Guest vehicles parked in a visitor space for more than 48 hours without a guest pass.

## 8. If Your Vehicle Is Towed

Larkfen Commons uses a contracted towing company, which can be reached 24 hours a day at (859) 555-0163.

Towed vehicles are taken to the impound lot at 1925 Pellwood Road, Lexington, KY 40511.

Vehicles can be released at the impound lot between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM daily.

All towing and impound fees are set by and paid directly to the towing company, not to Ostervale Property Management.

To release your vehicle, bring a photo ID and proof of ownership.

## 9. Accessible Parking

Accessible parking spaces are located near the clubhouse and at the entrance of each building.

Any vehicle parked in an accessible space must display a valid accessible parking placard or license plate.

Residents who need an accessible space closer to their building may make a request through the leasing office, and we will review it promptly.

## 10. Winter Weather

The parking lot and driveways are plowed when snow reaches 2 inches, and the sidewalks and building entrances are treated with ice melt.

When a plowing notice is posted on building doors and in the resident portal, please move your vehicle to a cleared area during the posted hours so the crew can clear your space.

Vehicles are not towed for failing to move during plowing, but spaces that cannot be reached may not be cleared.

## 11. Responsibility for Vehicles

Ostervale Property Management is not responsible for damage to or theft of vehicles or their contents while parked at Larkfen Commons.

Please lock your vehicle and do not leave valuables in plain view.

## 12. Questions

Questions about parking permits, carports or guest passes may be directed to the leasing office at (859) 555-0142 or office@ostervale.example.
