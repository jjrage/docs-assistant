# Resident FAQ

These are the questions Larkfen Commons residents ask us most often. The answers are short summaries. The document named in each answer has the full details, and if this FAQ and that document ever differ, the document controls.

## Rent and Payments

### When is rent due, and when is it late?

Rent is due on the 1st of each month. Rent received by 11:59 PM on the 5th is on time, and a $75 late charge is added on the 6th. See the Rent Payment Guide.

### How much can late charges add up to?

After the $75 initial late charge, $10 is added each day starting on the 7th, but total late charges never exceed $150 in one month. See the Fee Schedule.

### Can I pay rent with a credit card?

Yes. Credit card payments in the resident portal carry a processor fee of 2.95% of the payment, and debit card payments carry a $3.95 fee. E-check and autopay payments are free. See the Rent Payment Guide.

### Can I pay part of my rent now and the rest later?

Only with a written payment plan signed by the Community Manager. Ask in writing by the 3rd of the month. See the Rent Payment Guide.

### Can I pay in cash?

No. We do not accept cash at any time. See the Rent Payment Guide.

## Deposits

### How much is the security deposit?

The standard security deposit is $500. Applicants approved with conditions pay one full month's rent. See the Security Deposit Policy.

### When will I get my deposit back after I move out?

Within 30 days after you move out and return all keys, together with an itemized statement. See the Security Deposit Policy.

### Can my deposit cover my last month's rent?

No. The security deposit may not be used as last month's rent. See the Security Deposit Policy.

## Maintenance

### How do I request a repair?

Submit a request in the resident portal at any time. For emergencies after hours, call (859) 555-0199. See the Maintenance Requests and Emergencies Procedure.

### How fast will maintenance respond?

Emergencies get a call back within 30 minutes and a technician within 2 hours. Urgent requests are handled within 24 hours, routine requests within 3 business days, and cosmetic requests are scheduled within 10 business days. See the Maintenance Requests and Emergencies Procedure.

### My air conditioning stopped working. Is that an emergency?

It is an emergency when the outdoor temperature is 90°F or higher. Otherwise it is an urgent request handled within 24 hours. See the Maintenance Requests and Emergencies Procedure.

### Who changes the air filters?

Our maintenance team replaces heating and air conditioning filters every 3 months, in January, April, July and October, at no charge. See the Maintenance Requests and Emergencies Procedure.

### I locked myself out. What do I do?

On weekdays from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, come to the leasing office with a photo ID; the fee is $25. At all other times, call (859) 555-0199; the fee is $75. See the Maintenance Requests and Emergencies Procedure.

## Pets

### Can I have a pet?

Yes. Up to three pets are allowed per apartment, with no more than two dogs, and every pet must be registered before it arrives. See the Pet Policy effective March 1, 2026.

### What does it cost to have a pet?

Each pet requires a refundable pet deposit of $400 and monthly pet rent of $35. See the Pet Policy effective March 1, 2026 and the Fee Schedule.

### Is there a weight limit for dogs?

Yes. Dogs may weigh up to 75 pounds at full maturity. There is no weight limit for cats. See the Pet Policy effective March 1, 2026.

### Do assistance animals pay pet fees?

No. Assistance animals are not pets, and no pet deposit, pet rent, breed restriction or weight limit applies. See the Pet Policy.

## Parking

### How many cars can I register?

Up to two vehicles per apartment. The first permit is included in rent, and the second costs $20 per month. See the Parking Policy.

### Where should my guests park?

In spaces marked "VISITOR," for up to 48 hours. For longer stays, get a free guest pass from the leasing office, valid for up to 7 days. See the Parking Policy.

### My car was towed. Who do I call?

Call the contracted towing company at (859) 555-0163. Vehicles are released at 1925 Pellwood Road between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM daily. See the Parking Policy.

## Community Living

### What are the quiet hours?

From 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM Sunday through Thursday nights, and from 11:00 PM to 9:00 AM on Friday and Saturday nights. See the Community Rules.

### Can I grill on my balcony?

Only with an electric grill. Charcoal and propane grills are not allowed on balconies or patios or within 10 feet of a building. See the Community Rules.

### Is smoking allowed?

Not inside apartments, on balconies or patios, in common areas, or within 25 feet of a building. Use the covered gazebo near Building C. See the Community Rules.

### How long can a guest stay with me?

Up to 14 consecutive days, and no more than 30 days in any 12-month period, without written approval. See the Community Rules.

### How do I get rid of an old couch?

Schedule a bulk item pickup by 5:00 PM on the Tuesday before the 2nd or 4th Thursday of the month. The fee is $30 per item. See the Trash, Recycling and Bulk Item Pickup guide.

## Amenities

### When is the pool open?

From the Saturday of Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day, every day from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. See the Amenities Rules.

### Can I reserve the clubhouse for a party?

Yes. Reserve the clubhouse lounge at the leasing office 7 to 60 days ahead. The fee is $50 plus a $100 refundable damage deposit, with a limit of 25 guests. See the Amenities Rules.

## Insurance, Renewal and Moving Out

### Do I need renter's insurance?

Yes. Your policy must include at least $100,000 of personal liability coverage and list Ostervale Property Management as an interested party. See the Renter's Insurance Requirement.

### How much notice do I need to give before moving out?

At least 60 days before your lease end date, using the Notice to Vacate form in the resident portal. See the Lease Renewal and Notice to Vacate Procedure.

### What happens if I don't renew or give notice?

Your lease continues month to month at your current rent plus a $150 monthly premium. See the Lease Renewal and Notice to Vacate Procedure.

### Can I break my lease early?

Yes, with 60 days' written notice, rent through your early termination date, and an early termination fee equal to two months' rent. See the Lease Renewal and Notice to Vacate Procedure.

### When do I have to turn in my keys?

By 12:00 PM (noon) on your move-out date. Keys returned later are charged a $50 late key return fee. See the Move-Out Checklist and Deposit Return Guide.

## Office Information

### What are the leasing office hours?

Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Saturday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and closed on Sunday. See the Community Contacts Directory.
