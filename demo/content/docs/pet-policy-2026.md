# Pet Policy

This Pet Policy applies to all residents of Larkfen Commons, managed by Ostervale Property Management, and is effective March 1, 2026. It is part of your Residential Lease Agreement.

**This policy replaces the Pet Policy effective January 1, 2024 in its entirety.** Where the two policies differ, this 2026 policy controls.

## 1. What Changed in 2026

The main changes from the 2024 Pet Policy are summarized below for residents who already have pets.

| Topic | 2026 Pet Policy |
|---|---|
| Number of pets | Up to three pets per apartment, with no more than two dogs |
| Dog weight limit | Dogs may weigh up to 75 pounds at full maturity |
| Pet deposit | A refundable pet deposit of $400 is required for each pet |
| Monthly pet rent | Monthly pet rent is $35 per pet |
| Restricted breeds | The restricted breed list now includes only four breeds and their mixes |
| Pet waste fine | Failure to pick up pet waste results in a fine of $50 per occurrence |
| Unauthorized pet fee | An unauthorized pet fee of $250 is charged for each unregistered animal |

## 2. Transition for Pets Already Registered

Pet rent of $35 per pet applies to all pets, including pets registered before March 1, 2026, beginning with the rent due on April 1, 2026.

Pets registered before March 1, 2026 do not need to pay the difference between the old and new pet deposit amounts.

## 3. Pet Registration

No animal may be kept in any apartment, even temporarily, until the resident has signed a pet addendum and registered the animal with the leasing office.

Pets must be registered before they arrive at the apartment.

To register a pet, provide a recent photo of the pet, proof of current rabies vaccination, and a current city license for dogs and cats.

Vaccination records must be updated with the leasing office every year.

Pets belonging to guests may not visit or stay in any apartment.

## 4. Number of Pets

Up to three (3) pets are allowed per apartment, and no more than two (2) of them may be dogs.

Aquariums of 20 gallons or less are not counted as pets and do not require a pet deposit.

Birds, hamsters, guinea pigs and other small caged animals count as pets and must be registered.

## 5. Weight Limit

Dogs may weigh no more than 75 pounds at full maturity.

There is no weight limit for cats.

## 6. Restricted Breeds

The following dog breeds, and any mix that includes them, are not permitted at Larkfen Commons: Pit Bull Terriers, Rottweilers, Presa Canarios, and wolf hybrids.

The leasing office may ask for veterinary records to confirm a dog's breed.

## 7. Prohibited Animals

Reptiles, ferrets, primates, farm animals and venomous animals are not permitted.

## 8. Pet Deposit and Pet Rent

A refundable pet deposit of $400 is required for each pet.

The pet deposit is held separately from the security deposit and is returned at move-out, less any pet-related damage, as described in the Security Deposit Policy.

Monthly pet rent is $35 per pet and is due together with rent on the 1st of each month.

## 9. Behavior and Leash Rules

Dogs must be on a leash no longer than 6 feet whenever they are outside the apartment.

Cats must be kept indoors or on a leash when outside the apartment.

Pets may not be left tied or unattended on balconies, on patios, or anywhere in the common areas.

Pets are not allowed in the pool area, the fitness center or the clubhouse.

Excessive barking or other pet noise that disturbs neighbors is a violation of the Community Rules.

## 10. Pet Waste

Residents must pick up pet waste immediately, anywhere on the property, and place it in a pet waste station or a dumpster.

Pet relief areas with waste stations are located behind Building B and beside Building C.

Failure to pick up pet waste results in a fine of $50 per occurrence.

Litter boxes must be cleaned regularly, and used litter must be bagged and placed in a dumpster. Do not flush litter down toilets.

## 11. Unauthorized Pets

If an unregistered animal is found in an apartment, the resident will be charged an unauthorized pet fee of $250.

The resident must also register the animal and pay the pet deposit within 7 days, or remove the animal from the community.

Pet rent will be charged back to the date the animal arrived.

## 12. Damage, Complaints and Removal

Residents are fully responsible for any damage, injury or cleaning caused by their pets, including damage to carpets, doors, blinds and landscaping.

If a pet bites or threatens a person or another animal, or if repeated complaints are received, management may require the resident to remove the pet within 7 days of written notice.

## 13. Assistance Animals

Assistance animals for persons with disabilities are not pets under this policy.

No pet deposit, pet rent, breed restriction or weight limit applies to an assistance animal.

Requests for an assistance animal should be made through the leasing office. Owners of assistance animals must still pick up waste and keep the animal under control.
